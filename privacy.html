Closet is designed to process only information reasonably necessary to provide and operate its features.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Closet may access, collect, use, retain, and protect information when you install or use the Closet Discord application.

We aim to minimize data processing and use information only when reasonably necessary for application functionality, security, maintenance, and compliance.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, Closet may process information made available through Discord that is reasonably necessary to provide requested functionality.

  • Discord user IDs and usernames;
  • Server, channel, role, or application identifiers;
  • Application settings and configuration data;
  • Command inputs or information you intentionally provide; and
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Closet may be used to:

  • Provide requested application features;
  • Maintain settings and functionality;
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and security incidents;
  • Troubleshoot errors and improve reliability; and
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform requirements.

4. How Information Is Shared

Closet does not sell personal information or Discord API data. Information is not shared with third parties except where reasonably necessary to operate the application, where you direct or authorize the sharing, or where disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by applicable law.

When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it. Users may request deletion through the official support or contact method associated with Closet, subject to applicable law and legitimate security or technical requirements.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information handled by Closet. However, no online service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. Requests can be made through the official support or contact method associated with Closet.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Closet changes or as legal and operational requirements evolve. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Closet developer team through the official support or contact method associated with the Closet application.
